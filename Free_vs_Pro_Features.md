# Alarmo: Free vs. Pro (Freemium) Feature Breakdown

The core philosophy of this model is: **The Free version must be genuinely useful and fully functional for a casual user**, while **the Pro version should target "power users" who need intense productivity, bulletproof accountability, and deep customization.**

---

## ⏰ 1. Alarm Menu (Wake-up & Accountability)

The Alarm tab is the core of the app. Free users should reliably wake up, but Pro users get the "bulletproof" tools to ensure they never oversleep.

### Free Version

* **Active Alarms:** Up to 3 active alarms (enough for morning, evening, and a backup).
* **Sounds:** 3–5 standard, high-quality alarm tones (e.g., Radar, Clock Alarm).
* **Wake-Up Missions:** Basic cognitive missions (e.g., Math [Easy/Medium], Memory Match).
* **Snooze:** Standard snooze durations (5 min, 10 min) with default limits.

### Pro Version (Alarmo Pro)

* **Unlimited Alarms:** Create as many alarms as needed.
* **Premium Missions:** Physical and high-friction missions to force users out of bed:
  * *Barcode/QR Scanner* (e.g., scan toothpaste in the bathroom).
  * *Step Counter* (walk 30 steps to mute).
  * *Find Color Tiles / Typing.*
  * *Hard/Very Hard Difficulty* for cognitive games.
* **Mission Chaining:** Require the user to complete *multiple* missions in a row to turn off the alarm (e.g., Math + Barcode).
* **Accountability Shield (Hardcore Mode):**
  * Snooze limits and penalties (e.g., deduct in-app points or eventually hard-lock the snooze button).
  * "Prevent Power Off" & "Prevent Force Close" safety nets (monitoring logic).
* **Premium Audio:** Full access to all ambient, nature, and aggressive audio files + **Spotify Integration** (custom tracks).
* **Wake-up Analytics:** Track snooze history and time-to-wake metrics.

---

## ⏳ 2. Timer Menu (Pomodoro & Focus)

The timer shouldn't just be a clock; it should be a productivity engine. Free gets basic time-boxing; Pro gets severe focus enforcement and analytics.

### Free Version

* **Standard Pomodoro:** Fixed durations (e.g., 25-minute work, 5-minute break).
* **Basic Ambient Sound:** 1 or 2 standard focus sounds (e.g., White Noise, Rain).
* **Standard Interface:** The basic aesthetic timer ring.

### Pro Version (Alarmo Pro)

* **Custom Durations:** Fully adjustable work, short break, and long break intervals.
* **Strict / Deep Focus Mode:**
  * If the user leaves the app during a timer, the timer fails/cancels (burns their progress).
  * Screen forced to stay on (burn-in protection UI).
* **Premium Ambient Library:** Coffee shop, deep space, binaural beats, lofi hip-hop.
* **Auto-Flow:** Auto-start breaks when work finishes, and auto-start work when breaks finish.
* **Session Tagging & Analytics:** Tag focus sessions (e.g., "Coding", "Reading") and view weekly productivity charts.

---

## 📅 3. Plan Menu (Daily Schedule & Habits)

Habit tracking can be easily segmented by volume and depth. Free users can casually track a couple of goals, while Pro users can map out their entire life.

### Free Version

* **Daily Tasks:** Unlimited regular one-off tasks/reminders.
* **Habit Limit:** Maximum of **2 or 3 active habits** (e.g., "Drink Water", "Read").
* **Basic Streaks:** Showing the current consecutive streak for a habit.

### Pro Version (Alarmo Pro)

* **Unlimited Habits:** Track infinite daily/weekly habits.
* **Habit Alarms:** Bind a specific loud Alarm natively to a Habit (e.g., an alarm that rings at 8:00 PM and the mission is taking a photo of your gym bag).
* **Sub-tasks & Checklists:** Break down a scheduled item into smaller checkable steps.
* **Advanced Routine Grouping:** Group 5 habits into a "Morning Routine" block that tracks as a single entity.
* **Detailed Analytics:** Completion percentage calendars, mood correlations, and historical heat-maps.

---

## 🌍 4. Overlap Menu (Global Time Planner)

This is a niche but highly valuable feature for remote workers, freelancers, and global teams.

### Free Version

* **City Limit:** Add up to **3 global cities** (e.g., Local Time, New York, Tokyo).
* **Time Scrubbing:** Can use the interactive slider to convert times visually across those 3 cities.

### Pro Version (Alarmo Pro)

* **Unlimited Cities:** Build massive dashboards of timezones.
* **Overlap Analysis Engine:** The app automatically calculates the "optimal meeting times" where standard working hours (9 AM - 5 PM) overlap across all saved timezones.
* **Custom Working Hours:** Set custom availability for specific cities (e.g., "London team works 10am-6pm").
* **One-Tap Share:** A premium export button that instantly formats the overlapping times into a clean text snippet to paste into Slack/Email (e.g., *"Available at: 10:00 AM NY / 3:00 PM LDN"*).

## 💎 Summary for the Upsell Screen

When pitching the "Alarmo Pro" subscription to users inside the app, the marketing pillars should be:

1. **Never Oversleep Again:** (QR/Barcode missions, Accountability shield).
2. **Deep Work Analytics:** (Custom Pomodoro, strict focus modes).
3. **Limitless Tracking:** (Unlimited habits, unlimited global cities).
4. **Premium Enhancements:** (Spotify alarms, full sound library, overlap analysis).
